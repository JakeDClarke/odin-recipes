Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Guanciale
  • 4 Eggs
  • 100g Parmagianno Regianno
  • Black Pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the guanciale on a low heat until crispy
  2. Boil your pasta.
  3. Whisk 3 egg yolks and 1 full egg in a bowl until homogenous.
  4. Add cheese into egg mixture.
  5. Add pasta and 1/2 cup pasta water into egg mixture, stirring until creamy.
  6. Add guanciale to pasta, and top with pepper.
