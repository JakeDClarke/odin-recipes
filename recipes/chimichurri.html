Ingredients

  • Parsley
  • Shallots
  • Chilli
  • Dried Oregano
  • Garlic
  • Red Wine Vinegar
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Finely dice parsley, chilli, shallots, and Garlic.
  2. Combine in a bowl and add red wine vinegar, oregano, and salt.
  3. Stream in olive oil whilst mixing until emulsified.
  4. Adjust seasoning to taste.
